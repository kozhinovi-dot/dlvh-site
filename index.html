01

The company

Diamond Line Vacation Homes Rental L.L.C. is a Dubai company that lets fully furnished residential apartments as holiday homes and manages them on behalf of their owners.

Our work sits between the two sides of the same home. Guests want a place that is ready on arrival, clean, and simply explained. Owners want their property looked after, let sensibly and accounted for. We take care of both, from our office in Opus by Omniyat, Business Bay.

Plain terms

Every booking confirmation states the property, the dates of stay, the number of guests and the total price in AED.

Ready homes

Apartments are cleaned and made up with fresh linen and towels between stays, with the kitchen and essentials in place.

Answerable to owners

Owners receive reporting on bookings, income and the condition of their apartments.

02

What we do

The full cycle of a holiday home, handled by one team: from the first look at an apartment to the statement its owner receives.

  1. Holiday home rental

    Furnished apartments let for short stays, priced per apartment per night.

  2. Management for owners

    Day-to-day running of an apartment on its owner's behalf.

  3. Apartment set-up

    Inventory, documentation and preparation before a home takes its first guests.

  4. Listings & reservations

    Presentation of each home on booking channels and handling of every reservation.

  5. Guest communication

    Answers before arrival, during the stay and after departure.

  6. Arrivals & departures

    Check-in instructions, access and check-out coordinated for each stay.

  7. Housekeeping & linen

    Cleaning between stays, fresh linen and towels, consumables restocked.

  8. Maintenance & reporting

    Repairs coordinated with contractors, and owners kept informed throughout.

03

How an apartment comes to us

  1. i

    Assess

    We look at the apartment, its building and its likely demand, and agree terms with the owner.

  2. ii

    Set up

    The home is documented, equipped and photographed, and readied for its first guests.

  3. iii

    Host

    Reservations, guest messages, arrivals, cleaning and upkeep are handled by our team.

  4. iv

    Account

    The owner receives a clear statement of bookings, income and costs.

  • Cleaning, linen and maintenance coordinated for you

Pricing and payment

  • Per apartment, not per person. The nightly rate covers the whole apartment for up to the stated number of guests.
  • VAT included. The published rate already includes VAT; no service charge is added.
  • Dubai tourism fee. AED 30 per apartment per night is collected at the property, as at every property in the city.
  • Prepayment. A reservation is confirmed against a prepayment equal to the total of the guest's booking confirmation. The guest receives a personal payment page whose invoice reference carries the reservation number.
  • Payment methods. Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay and Google Pay, in AED, on secure payment pages operated by Arab Financial Services. Card details are never collected or stored on this website.
  • Free cancellation. On flexible rates, cancellation is free up to 5 days before arrival, with a full refund to the same card — see Cancellation & Refunds.
  • New Year period. Rates over Christmas and New Year are set separately and are higher than the ranges above.

Rates are indicative and updated with demand. The binding price for specific dates is the one confirmed at the time of booking. Last updated 2 September 2026.

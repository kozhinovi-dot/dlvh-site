Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains how Diamond Line Vacation Homes Rental L.L.C. (“the Company”, “we”) handles personal data collected through the website dlvh.ae, the Owner Portal at dlvh.ae/owners and the Diamond Line Owners mobile app.

Data we collect

The public website does not require registration. We collect personal data only when you choose to send it to us:

  • the name, email address and message you enter in the enquiry form;
  • the content of any email you send to our published address.

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies on this website, and we do not track visitors across other websites.

Owner Portal and mobile app

The Owner Portal and the Diamond Line Owners app are available only to property owners who have a management agreement with the Company. Accounts are created by the Company by invitation; the app does not offer public sign-up. For each owner we keep:

  • name, email address and an encrypted password;
  • details of the managed property, its bookings, monthly statements, expenses, work records and documents;
  • the bank account details the owner enters for payouts, and a history of changes to them;
  • records of documents viewed and signed, including the time and the network address of the signature.

This data is used only to manage the owner’s property, prepare statements, make payouts and keep the records required by our agreement and by law. Face ID or Touch ID, if the owner turns it on, is handled by the device; the app never receives biometric data. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Portal data is stored with a cloud database provider that processes it on our behalf, and notification emails are sent from our email provider. Owners can ask for their account and data to be deleted by writing to info@dlvh.ae; records that we must keep by law (for example statements and payouts) are retained for the period the law requires.

How we use it

Your data is used solely to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to discuss and administer property management services. We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for unsolicited marketing.

Processing and storage

Enquiries submitted through the form are transmitted by a third-party form delivery service and arrive in the Company’s email inbox, which is hosted by an external email provider. These providers process the data on our behalf for the purpose of delivering and storing correspondence. If your browser cannot submit the form, it will instead open your own email application, in which case the message is sent directly by you.

Correspondence is retained for as long as necessary to handle the enquiry and to meet the Company’s record-keeping obligations under applicable United Arab Emirates law.

Disclosure

We do not disclose personal data to third parties except where required by law or by a competent authority in the United Arab Emirates, or where disclosure is necessary to provide a service you have requested.

Your rights

You may request access to the personal data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask us to delete it, subject to any retention obligations that apply to us. Requests should be sent to info@dlvh.ae.

External links

This website may link to third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those services; please review their policies separately.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.

The problem
we solve

A client wants Dubai. Not a standard room — a two-bedroom on the Palm for a family of five, a villa with a pool for a group, a residence for a month. The booking platforms show retail prices with nothing left for you, and the wholesale systems your agency already uses barely carry this kind of property at all.

So the request turns into an afternoon of writing to operators you have never worked with, in a city you cannot check, with no one to call if something goes wrong on arrival.

Send it to us instead. We are in Dubai, we hold the licence, and we work with the properties directly.

04

How it
works

  1. You send one request Dates, guests, area, budget. Two lines are enough — the form below takes thirty seconds and everything except the essentials is optional.
  2. We acknowledge it with a request number Every request gets an ID. You quote it, we quote it, nothing gets lost in a thread.
  3. We source Our own units first, then our operator network, then the wider market if the request needs it.
  4. You get three to five options Photos, exact property, dates, net price, deposit and cancellation terms. Clean enough to forward to your client as it is.
  5. You confirm, we book We contract the property and handle payment. You deal with one counterparty and one invoice, not five operators.
  6. We stay on the ground Check-in, keys, questions during the stay, anything that goes wrong. We are in Dubai and we are the ones who answer.

07

Questions

We already work with a DMC. Why add another supplier?

Most DMCs are built around hotels, tours and transfers. We are built around apartments, villas and residences — the requests your DMC usually comes back empty on. Send us one of those and compare.

How fast do you answer?

We acknowledge every request with a number as soon as it arrives, and we come back with options the same working day for standard requests. If a request is urgent, say so — we will tell you honestly whether we can meet the deadline rather than let it run.

Can we see your inventory?

There is no catalogue to browse, deliberately. Availability in Dubai changes daily and a static list would be wrong by the time you read it. Send a request and you get what is genuinely available for those dates, checked before we send it.

What does it cost us to try?

Nothing. No agreement, no registration, no minimum. Send one live request and judge us on the answer.

Who is responsible for the guest?

Once a booking is confirmed, we are — for the property, the check-in and anything that happens during the stay. Your relationship with your client stays yours; the operational risk in Dubai becomes ours.

What if the property falls through?

We replace it with an equivalent or better property at no additional cost to you, and we tell you the moment we know rather than the day before arrival. This is written into our partner terms.

How do payments work?

We invoice your agency in AED and you pay by bank transfer or card. We never ask your client for card details, and we do not collect payment from them directly unless you specifically ask us to.

Do you offer credit terms?

Not at the start. New partners prepay on confirmation. Once we have a booking history together, payment terms are open to discussion — and we will say yes or no plainly rather than leave it vague.
